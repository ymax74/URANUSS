# URANUSS
The problem of improving the ephemerides of the major satellites of Uranus based on ground-based astrometric observations remains relevant. We present the positions of the Uranian satellites in the Gaia DR3 catalog reference frame, characterized by an internal accuracy of 20–50 mas for modern observations obtained with the 40-cm Pulkovo telescope at the Assy-Turgen Observatory, and 80 mas for scans of photographic plates taken with the Normal Astrograph (1919 and 1930) and digitized with the NAROO facility (Paris Observatory). An analysis of the O–C residuals for various combinations of 'planet+satellites' dynamical models reveals signs of systematic deviations, which may be explained by the imperfections of the current ephemerides.
